Privacy Policy

SmartMed Cycle is designed with patient privacy and safety as our highest priority. We believe healthcare tools should respect your confidentiality.

1. No Account Required

You do not need to create an account, register, or provide your name, phone number, or national ID to use SmartMed Cycle.

2. Medicine Details & Label Photos

  • Text inputs: Medicine details and notes you type are processed solely in real-time to generate plain-language explanations.
  • Label photos: Photos uploaded to the app are processed temporarily to extract text instructions and are not stored permanently or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Privacy reminder: You are welcome and encouraged to physically cover or crop out any personal identifiers (name, address, IC number) on prescription labels before uploading.

3. Local Device Storage

We use your browser’s local storage (localStorage) only to remember your preferred language selection (Bahasa Melayu, English, or 中文) for your convenience.

4. User Feedback & Safety Reporting

If you choose to use the "🚩 Report issue" feature, only the flagged AI text and your selected feedback category are submitted to help our team improve clinical prompt safety. No personal identity is attached to feedback reports.

5. Questions & Contact

If you have any questions about privacy or how your information is handled, please email us at smartmedcycle@gmail.com.
