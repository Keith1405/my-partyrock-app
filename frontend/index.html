💊 SmartMed Cycle

Your personal medicine companion — understand, check, ask, and return responsibly.

  • 🔎 Understand My Medicine — snap or type a label to get a plain-language summary
  • 🛡️ Check My Precautions — spot what to watch for and what to ask
  • 💬 Ask About My Medicines — chat with SmartMed Help, any time
  • ♻️ Return Unused Medicines — find out how to return it responsibly

⚠️ Heads up: SmartMed Cycle is a preparation tool — not a substitute for pharmacist or prescriber advice. Always compare outputs with your original label.

♻️ Section 4 · Return Unused Medicines

🗑️ You can return any unwanted medicine — prescription medicines, over-the-counter products, vitamins, supplements, or medicines found at home.

📍 Enter the medicine details and your location below and we'll help you prepare your return and point you to the official myMediSAFE directory.

🔴 Not sure if you should stop taking a medicine? Check with your pharmacist or prescriber before returning it.

🔗 Official return directory: myMediSAFE — search by state, city, or postcode.

📦 Optional. Upload a clear photo of the labelled packaging. 🔍 The label must be visible and readable. ⚠️ If the image shows only unlabelled pills with no readable label, the assistant will note this and will not attempt to identify the medicine from appearance alone.
