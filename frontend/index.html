The following medicines are safely accepted at all participating collection boxes:

Hard & softgel

Inhalers
Puffers, disks

Liquid Meds

Eye Drops
Bottles / ampoules
👃
Nasal Sprays
Nasal pumps
🕯️
Suppositories
& Pessaries
💉
Insulin Pens
Pens & cartridges
🩹
Patches
Transdermal patches
✉️
Sachets
Powders & ORS
🧴
Topical Preparations
Creams, gels & ointments
✕

What cannot be disposed?

Do not drop these into collection boxes:

✂️
Sharp Materials
Needles & lancets (Use sharps bin)
📟
Medical Devices
Glucometers & BP meters
🩹
Clinical Wastes
Used gauze, diapers, IV sets
🦿
Prosthetics
Artificial limbs & braces
Environmental Note: Never flush medicines down the toilet or throw them into household trash.
