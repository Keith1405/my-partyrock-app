Accessibility Statement

SmartMed Cycle aims to make medicine information easier to understand for patients and caregivers, including older adults and people with disabilities.

Using this website

  • Use your browser’s zoom controls to make text and images larger.
  • You can type medicine details instead of uploading a photo.
  • If any step is difficult, ask someone you trust to help you use the website.

Tell us about a difficulty

If you have trouble reading information, using a control or completing a step, please email us at support@smartmedcycle.org.

Please tell us which page you were using, what you were trying to do and, if possible, your device and browser. Do not include personal medical information or unedited prescription photos.

We welcome suggestions and will use your feedback to improve accessibility. We have not yet completed a formal accessibility audit.

For questions about your medicines, speak with your pharmacist.
