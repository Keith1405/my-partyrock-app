Welcome to SmartMed Cycle. By accessing or using this website, you agree to these simple terms designed to keep patients safe.

🚨 Medical Emergency Notice

SmartMed Cycle is not an emergency service. In Malaysia, dial 999 immediately for medical emergencies. Outside Malaysia, call your local emergency service.

1. Preparation Tool Only — Not Medical Advice

SmartMed Cycle is an educational preparation tool designed to help patients understand general medicine labels and organize questions for their healthcare providers. It is not a substitute for professional medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. Always Verify with Original Label & Pharmacist

  • Always compare generated information against the official label physically attached to your medicine packaging.
  • Never start, stop, or alter your prescribed medicine dosage without first consulting your doctor or pharmacist.
  • AI-assisted extraction may have limitations; your pharmacist is the ultimate expert on your medications.

3. Medicine Disposal & Returns

Information on drop-off bins and collection points is provided for community guidance. Please confirm operating hours and accepted item categories directly with the facility before your visit.

4. Permitted Use

This service is free for personal, non-commercial use by patients, caregivers, and families. Automated scraping or misuse of the underlying services is strictly prohibited.
