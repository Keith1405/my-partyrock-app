About SmartMed Cycle

Understanding your medicines should feel simpler.

SmartMed Cycle helps patients and caregivers understand medicine information, prepare questions for their pharmacist and find guidance on returning unwanted medicines.

Whether you type medicine details or upload a label photo, our aim is to make information clearer and help you feel more prepared for your next pharmacist conversation.

We believe that better understanding and responsible medicine disposal are small steps towards a healthier community.
SmartMed Cycle is a preparation tool. Always check your original medicine label and confirm medicine advice with your pharmacist.
